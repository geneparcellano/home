How it works

Four steps from scattered files to a tidy archive — with a full preview before anything moves.

Step 1

Drop a folder

Drag a folder into Grammafold or use Choose Folder… (⌘O). The app scans recursively with no depth limit.

Step 2

Scan your archive

Grammafold reads EXIF and video metadata from every supported file. Progress is shown as thousands of files are indexed.

Step 3

Preview every move

Review the full organization plan before confirming — including year folders, date subfolders, fallback dates, and collisions. Choose your preferred date format.

Step 4

Organize and undo

Confirm to move files into date subfolders inside your selected folder. Changed your mind? Undo restores everything to its original layout.

From photo chaos to organized archive

Turn scattered imports into a clear, date-based structure you can browse in Finder.

Before

Thousands of photos scattered in one folder — hard to browse and impossible to find by date.

After

A clear year and date folder structure in Finder. When files span multiple years, folders become YYYY/MM-DD-YYYY/.

Choose your date format

Pick from multiple date folder formats before organizing. Default is MM-DD-YYYY.

Undo restores everything

Not happy with the result? Undo moves all files back to their original locations.

Built for real archives

Everything you need to organize safely — nothing you don’t.

In-place organization

Files move into date subfolders inside the folder you select. You keep normal Finder folders you own.

EXIF and metadata

Reads photo EXIF and video metadata to determine when each file was taken.

Preview before organizing

Review every planned move — including fallback dates and collision handling — before confirming.

Collisions and fallbacks

Same-name files get safe suffixes. Missing metadata falls back to file dates, clearly labeled in the preview.

Undo support

Restore your previous folder layout if you change your mind after organizing.

Privacy-first

All processing on your Mac. No uploads, no analytics, no account, no tracking.

Remove empty folders

Optional cleanup removes empty folders after organizing. Off by default — enable in settings.
