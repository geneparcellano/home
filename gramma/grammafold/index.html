How it works

Four steps from scattered files to a tidy archive — with a full preview before anything moves.

  1. 1.

    Drop a folder

    Drag a folder into Grammafold or use Choose Folder… (⌘O). The app scans recursively with no depth limit.

  2. 2.

    Scan and preview

    Grammafold reads EXIF and video metadata to determine dates, then shows a preview of every planned move.

  3. 3.

    Organize in place

    Confirm to move files into date subfolders inside your selected folder. Default format: MM-DD-YYYY.

  4. 4.

    Undo anytime

    Changed your mind? Undo the organization to restore your previous layout.

Built for real archives

Everything you need to organize safely — nothing you don’t.

In-place organization

Files move into date subfolders inside the folder you select. You keep normal Finder folders you own.

EXIF and metadata

Reads photo EXIF and video metadata to determine when each file was taken.

Preview before organizing

Review every planned move — including fallback dates and collision handling — before confirming.

Collisions and fallbacks

Same-name files get safe suffixes. Missing metadata falls back to file dates, clearly labeled in the preview.

Undo support

Restore your previous folder layout if you change your mind after organizing.

Privacy-first

All processing on your Mac. No uploads, no analytics, no account, no tracking.

Remove empty folders

Optional cleanup removes empty folders after organizing. Off by default — enable in settings.
