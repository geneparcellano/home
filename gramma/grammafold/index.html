How it works

Four steps from messy folders to a tidy archive — with a preview before anything moves.

  1. 1

    Choose a folder

    Select the folder containing your scattered photos and videos.

  2. 2

    Scan your photos and videos

    Grammafold reads metadata recursively to determine each file’s date.

  3. 3

    Preview the organization plan

    Review exactly where every file will go before confirming.

  4. 4

    Organize your archive

    Files move into date-based folders. Undo if you change your mind.
