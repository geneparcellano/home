How it works

Four simple steps. You always preview before anything moves.

Step 1

Drop a folder

Drag a folder into Grámmafold, or click Choose Folder.

Step 2

Grámmafold scans your folder

It finds the date for each photo and video. Large folders may take a moment.

Step 3

Preview every move

See exactly where every file will go before you confirm.

Step 4

Organize and undo

Confirm to move files into date folders. Changed your mind? Undo puts everything back.

From photo chaos to organized archive

See the difference in Finder.

Before

Photos and videos scattered in one folder.

After

A clear folder for each date, organized by year — easy to browse in Finder.

Choose your date format

Pick the date style that works best for you before organizing.

Undo restores everything
