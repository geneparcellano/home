Getting started

  1. Open Grámmafold and drop a folder onto the window, or choose Choose Folder… (⌘O).
  2. Wait for the scan. Grámmafold recursively searches your folder (no depth limit) and reads EXIF and video metadata from supported files.
  3. Review the preview. See exactly where each file will move, including any fallback dates or name collisions.
  4. Organize. Confirm to move files into date subfolders inside your selected folder. Default format is MM-DD-YYYY; you can change the format in settings.
  5. Undo if needed. Use undo to restore your previous folder layout.

Your files remain on your Mac throughout. Grámmafold does not upload anything.

Mac App Store

Grámmafold is distributed through the Mac App Store. Updates are delivered automatically via the App Store when available.

Frequently asked questions

  • How do I organize my photos?

    Drop a folder into Grámmafold or use Choose Folder… (⌘O). After the scan completes, review the preview showing where each file will go, then confirm to organize. Files move into date subfolders within the folder you selected.

  • Does Grámmafold upload my photos?

    No. Grámmafold works entirely on your Mac. Your photos, videos, filenames, and metadata never leave your computer. There are no cloud uploads, no servers, and no network transmission of your files.

  • Does Grámmafold modify my photos?

    No. Grámmafold only moves files into organized folders. It does not edit, compress, convert, or alter the contents of your photos or videos.

  • What are fallback dates?

    Grámmafold reads dates from EXIF and video metadata first. When metadata is missing or unreadable, it falls back to the file’s creation or modification date on disk. Files using fallback dates are clearly labeled in the preview so you can review them before organizing.

  • What happens if files have the same name?

    Grámmafold uses safe collision handling. If a file with the same name already exists in the destination folder, it appends a unique suffix instead of overwriting. All collision resolutions appear in the preview before you confirm.

  • What file formats are supported?

    Photos: .jpg, .jpeg, .heic, .heif, .tif, .tiff, .png, .raf, .dng, .cr2, .nef, .arw
    Videos: .mov, .mp4, .m4v, .avi, .mkv

  • Why are some files marked unsupported?

    Files with extensions outside the supported format list, or files whose metadata cannot be read, are marked unsupported in the preview. They are not moved during organization. Non-media files in your folder are ignored.

  • Can I undo after organizing?

    Yes. After organizing, use the undo action to restore files to their original locations and folder layout.

  • What does “Remove empty folders” do?

    When enabled in settings, Grámmafold removes folders that become empty after organizing. This option is off by default. The preference is stored locally in UserDefaults on your Mac.

  • What macOS version is required?

    Grámmafold requires macOS 14.0 or later.

  • How does folder access work?

    Grámmafold runs in the macOS sandbox. It can only access folders you explicitly select through the folder picker (⌘O) or by dragging a folder into the app. It cannot browse your Mac without your permission.
