Getting started

  1. Drop a folder into Grámmafold, or click Choose Folder.
  2. Wait for the scan to finish.
  3. Review the preview to see where each file will go.
  4. Organize when you’re ready.
  5. Undo if you want to put everything back.

Your files stay on your Mac. Grámmafold never uploads anything.

Frequently asked questions

  • Does Grámmafold upload my photos?

    No. Everything stays on your Mac. Your photos and videos never leave your computer.

  • Does Grámmafold change my photos?

    No. Grámmafold only moves files into folders. It does not edit or alter your photos and videos.

  • What if a photo has no date?

    Grámmafold uses the date embedded in the photo or video when available. If none is found, it uses the file’s date on your Mac. These are clearly marked in the preview so you can review them first.

  • What if two files have the same name?

    Grámmafold never overwrites files. If a name already exists, it adds a number to make the name unique. You’ll see this in the preview before organizing.

  • Can I undo after organizing?

    Yes. Undo puts all files back where they were.

  • What file types are supported?

    JPEG, HEIC, PNG, TIFF, RAW files from major cameras, MOV, MP4, and other common photo and video formats.

  • What Mac do I need?

    Grámmafold requires macOS 14 or later.
