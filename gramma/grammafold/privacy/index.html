Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 21, 2026

Grammafold is built with privacy at its core. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

  • Grammafold does not collect personal data
  • Grammafold does not upload files
  • Photos, videos, and metadata remain on your Mac
  • No analytics
  • No tracking
  • No advertising identifiers
  • Files are accessed only when explicitly selected by you

Overview

Grammafold is a macOS application that helps you organize photos and videos into a chronological folder structure. The app runs entirely on your computer. We do not operate servers that receive your files or personal information.

Information we do not collect

Grammafold does not collect, store, or transmit any of the following:

  • Personal information such as your name, email address, or location
  • Photos, videos, or any media file contents
  • Photo or video metadata (such as EXIF data)
  • Usage analytics or behavioral data
  • Device identifiers or advertising identifiers
  • Crash reports or diagnostic data sent to us

How Grammafold accesses your files

Grammafold accesses files only when you explicitly choose a folder to organize. The app reads file metadata locally to determine dates and previews the organization plan on your Mac. Files are moved only after you confirm the plan.

Grammafold does not access files in the background, scan directories without your permission, or read files outside the folder you select.

No network transmission

Grammafold does not upload your photos, videos, or metadata to any server. The app does not require an internet connection to function and does not communicate with external services for its core features.

No tracking or analytics

Grammafold does not include analytics SDKs, tracking pixels, or advertising frameworks. We do not monitor how you use the app, and we do not share any information with third parties.

No account required

Grammafold does not require you to create an account or sign in. There is no user database and no authentication system associated with the app.

Third-party services

Grammafold is distributed through the Mac App Store, which is operated by Apple. Apple’s privacy practices for app downloads and purchases are governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy. Grammafold itself does not integrate with any third-party services.

Children’s privacy

Grammafold does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Because the app collects no personal data, there is no data to access, modify, or delete.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of Grammafold after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at support@[my-domain].
