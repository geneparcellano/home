Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Grámmafold keeps your photos and videos on your Mac. Here is what we do — and don’t do — with your information.

  • No personal information collected
  • Photos and videos never uploaded
  • Everything stays on your Mac
  • Only folders you choose are opened

Your data

Grámmafold organizes photos and videos into date-based folders on your device. We do not run servers and receive nothing from you — no name, email, location, filenames, or usage data.

The app only opens folders you select. It reads dates to build a preview and moves files only after you confirm. Grámmafold does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking. No account is required.

Settings

Your preferences are saved on your Mac only.

Mac App Store purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. That process does not involve your photos or videos. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Children

Grámmafold is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal data.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted here with a new date.
