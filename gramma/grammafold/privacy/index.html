Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Grámmafold is built with privacy at its core. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

  • We do not collect your personal information
  • We do not upload your photos or videos
  • Everything stays on your Mac
  • No tracking or analytics
  • Grámmafold only opens folders you choose

Your files stay on your Mac

Grámmafold organizes photos and videos into date-based folders. All of this happens on your device. We do not run servers, and we do not receive your files or personal information.

What we don’t collect

Grámmafold does not collect your name, email, location, photos, videos, filenames, or how you use the app. Nothing is sent to us.

How Grámmafold uses your files

Grámmafold only accesses folders you select. It reads photo and video dates to organize your files, shows you a preview, and moves files only after you confirm. It does not access other folders on your Mac.

No uploads or tracking

Grámmafold does not upload your files to the cloud. It does not include analytics, advertising, or tracking. No account is required.

Settings saved on your Mac

Grámmafold saves your preferences locally on your Mac, such as the “Remove empty folders” option. Nothing is synced elsewhere.

Mac App Store purchases

If you purchase Grámmafold through the Mac App Store, Apple handles the transaction. That process does not involve your photos or videos. See Apple’s Privacy Policy for details.

Children

Grámmafold is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal data.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted here with a new date.
