Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Grámmafold is built with privacy at its core. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

  • Grámmafold does not collect personal information
  • Grámmafold does not upload photos, filenames, or folder contents
  • Photos, videos, and metadata remain on your Mac
  • No analytics, tracking, or telemetry
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No cloud sync or third-party SDKs
  • Files are accessed only in folders you explicitly select

Overview

Grámmafold is a macOS application that organizes photos and videos into date-based folders using EXIF and media metadata. All processing happens on your device. We do not operate servers that receive your files or personal information.

Information we do not collect

Grámmafold does not collect, store, or transmit:

  • Personal information such as your name, email address, or location
  • Photos, videos, or any media file contents
  • Photo or video metadata (such as EXIF data)
  • Filenames or folder structures
  • Usage analytics or behavioral data
  • Device identifiers or advertising identifiers
  • Crash reports or diagnostic data sent to us

Local processing only

Grámmafold accesses files only in folders you explicitly select through the macOS folder picker (⌘O) or by dragging a folder into the app. The app reads metadata locally to determine dates, shows a preview on your Mac, and moves files only after you confirm.

Grámmafold does not access files in the background, scan directories without your permission, or read files outside the folder you select.

Folder access and sandbox

Grámmafold runs in the macOS App Sandbox with com.apple.security.app-sandbox and com.apple.security.files.user-selected.read-write entitlements. This means the app can only read and write files in folders you grant access to through the system folder picker or drag-and-drop.

Metadata

Grámmafold reads EXIF and video metadata, and may read file creation dates from the filesystem, solely to organize your files locally. This information is not transmitted off your device.

The app’s privacy manifest declares use of the file timestamp API for reading file creation dates locally.

Data stored on your Mac

The only preference Grámmafold persists is the “Remove empty folders” toggle, stored locally in UserDefaults on your Mac. No other settings or data are synced or transmitted.

No network transmission of your media

Grámmafold does not upload your photos, videos, metadata, or filenames to any server. The app does not require an internet connection for its core features and does not communicate with external services to organize your files.

No tracking or analytics

Grámmafold does not include analytics SDKs, tracking pixels, or advertising frameworks. We do not monitor how you use the app, and we do not share any information with third parties for advertising or analytics.

No account required

Grámmafold does not require you to create an account or sign in. There is no user database associated with the app.

Mac App Store purchases

Release builds use StoreKit 2 AppTransaction to validate your Mac App Store purchase with Apple. This communication is between your Mac and Apple’s servers for license verification only. It does not involve your photos, videos, or folder contents.

Apple’s privacy practices for app downloads and purchases are governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Third-party services

Grámmafold does not integrate third-party analytics, advertising, or cloud storage SDKs. The app is distributed through the Mac App Store, which is operated by Apple.

Children’s privacy

Grámmafold is not directed at children under 13. Because the app collects no personal data, there is no data to access, modify, or delete.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact
